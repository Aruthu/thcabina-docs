Support

Questions about the theme, setup help and bug reports. We reply within two business days. What is covered and what is not is set out in the support policy.

Two checks that solve most reports, in case one of them is yours:

  • Product options. In the theme editor, Theme settings → Product options: THCabina needs to know which option position is your color and which is your size. Blocks that depend on an undeclared option render nothing. (Guide, section 3)
  • The try-on area is empty. That is by design until you install a virtual try-on app and add its block to the product page. (Guide, section 7)
