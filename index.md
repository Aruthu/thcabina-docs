# THCabina

A Shopify theme for apparel stores that offer virtual try-on: a dedicated slot on the product page,
color and size options you declare yourself, and every product image on one screen.

Theme version **1.0.0**.

## Documentation

- **[Theme guide](guide.html)** — installation, product options, metafields, theme settings, the
  product page, and how to add your virtual try-on app.
- **[Support policy](support-policy.html)** — what we support, what we do not, and how fast we reply.

## Support

- **[Contact us](support.html)** — the support form. We reply within two business days.

## Before you buy

THCabina reserves a place on the product page for the virtual try-on app of your choice. **The theme
does not include a try-on app**, and until you install one that area stays empty and the try-on
button does not appear on product cards. Everything else in the theme works with no apps installed at
all. See [section 1 of the guide](guide.html#1-before-you-buy).
